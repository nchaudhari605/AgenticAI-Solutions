MRI Lumbar Spine Policy

Approval Criteria:

1. Patient must have lower back pain lasting greater than 6 weeks.

2. One of the following diagnosis:
   - Radiculopathy
   - Neurological deficit
   - Suspected disc herniation
   - Lumbar Radiculopathy

3. Conservative treatment history attempted:
   - Physical therapy
   - Medication management

4. Clinical documentation must include:
   - Diagnosis
   - Provider notes
   - Treatment history

Decision Rules:

If all criteria are met:
Recommendation = APPROVE

If documentation is incomplete:
Recommendation = PEND

If criteria are not met:
Recommendation = DENY
